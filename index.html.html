⚠ Keep biologicals alive

Bio-Capsules and microbe products are stable until mixed. Never pre-blend hopper products the day before — talc/graphite base is fine, but Bio-Capsule contents belong at the planter, not overnight in the shed. Water for tank-mixes: neutralize chlorine with BioFlex before adding CycleStrike or any microbial product.
