ProHarvest Seeds — 2026-27 Sales Policies

Effective September 1, 2026

Corn & Soybean Packaging

  • All corn hybrids priced per 80,000 kernel bag (1 unit). Available in 36 or 50 unit Black Boxes unless seed size prevents it.
  • Soybeans priced per 140,000 seeds / unit. Available in 140K paper bag, 40-unit mini-bulk, and 40-unit Black Boxes (Black Box soybeans must be treated with proTect DI or Ultra).

Replant Policy

  • Seed planted prior to each county's insurable multi-peril planting date qualifies for replant at 50% of retail.
  • Free replant seed corn and proTect-treated soybeans furnished up to the first 25% of the original order. Beyond that: 25% of full retail.
  • Soybeans must be treated with proTect DI or Ultra to qualify — untreated soybeans are invoiced at 25% of retail.
  • Covers loss from natural causes (flooding, frost, crusting, animal feeding). Does not cover herbicide misapplication.
  • One replant credit per season. Replant seed does not qualify for further discounts or programs.

Return Policy

  • No returns of Seed Corn after June 15, 2027; Soybeans after June 30, 2027.
  • Returns > 10% of original order are subject to restocking charge.
  • No returns or cancellation of treated soybeans or Sold-Out products after Jan 15, 2027.
  • No returns of partial, opened, taped, hole-damaged, rodent-damaged, or dirty bags.

Financing (First Mid & John Deere Financial)

  • First Mid 0% — Corn & Soybeans only, order by Jan 15, 2027. Volume & early-order discounts still apply. $1,000 min. Due in full Dec 2027.
  • JDF 1.9% Fixed — Corn & Soybeans only, order by Jan 15, 2027. Volume/early-order apply; early-pay cash does not. $2,500 min. Due Dec 2027.
  • JDF Prime -1% — Corn & Soybeans only, order by Mar 31, 2027. Volume/early-order apply; early-pay cash does not. $2,500 min. Due Dec 2027.
  • First Mid Prime +0.25% — All Crops. Volume/early-order apply; early-pay cash does not. $1,000 min. Due Dec 2027.
  • JDF Prime +6.10% — Volume/early-order apply; early-pay cash does not. $1,000 min. Due Dec 2027.

Fine print

  • Financing apps must be submitted to First Mid or JDF within 7 days of order placement.
  • Credit card payments > $2,000 incur a 3.5% usage charge.
  • All 1st-year customers pay prior to pickup/delivery unless credit pre-approved. Previous accounts must be paid in full before current-year shipments.
  • All accounts due in full by June 30, 2027 or 30 days from shipment, whichever is later.
  • Black boxes not returned invoiced at $550 each; damaged/missing parts at replacement cost.
